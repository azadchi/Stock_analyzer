When analyzing Iranian stocks (TSE), follow this framework:

1. ICHIMOKU: Check price vs cloud, Tenkan/Kijun cross, distance from Kijun (over 15% = high correction risk)
2. RSI(14): Over 70 overbought, under 30 oversold, check divergence
3. MACD(12-26-9): Signal line cross, histogram direction
4. STOCHASTIC(14-3-3): Under 20 oversold, over 80 overbought
5. BOLLINGER BANDS(20): Price at upper band = correction risk, squeeze = breakout coming
6. EMA: EMA21 > EMA50 > EMA200 = bullish alignment
7. VOLUME: Above 30-day average + bullish candle = confirmed uptrend

Output format:
- Overall trend and strength
- Signal: BUY / SELL / WAIT
- Entry point
- Target 1 and Target 2
- Stop loss (below last swing low or Kijun)
- Risk/Reward ratio (minimum 1:2)
- Status of each indicator

Risk rules:
- Mandatory stop loss
- Max risk per trade: 5-10%
- Min 3 confirming indicators before entry
- Iran market 5% daily limit applies
- Max 20-30% capital per stock
